# Known limitations and unverified behavior

- **Research only.** Native compilation and offline tool tests do not establish strategy quality or broker correctness. MT5 fixture execution, real-tick backtests and forward demo are not yet run. No performance statistics or optimized settings are supplied.
- **Broker clock must be verified.** Fixed, US-rule and EU-rule broker schedules are supported. Bespoke schedules or historical broker clock changes require another tested adapter. Supported calendar years are 2007–2099; future rules are extrapolations. Ambiguous/nonexistent broker or configured session wall times fail closed, so some transition-hour bars/sessions are intentionally skipped.
- **First-tick processing and gaps.** Attachment skips the already completed candle. Missing bars, blocked required filters and unsent restart setups are skipped/invalidated without catch-up execution. This deliberately sacrifices some research opportunities to avoid retrospective entries. History/ATR must be ready on the processing tick; the EA does not retry that signal later.
- **Observed-tick actions.** There is no timer-based trading. Session-end flattening, news/extreme cancellation, risk checks and trade reconciliation need observed chart-symbol ticks. Sparse quotes, disconnection, unsupported exact expiry, market closure, stop/freeze restrictions and request failure can leave exposure. A limit can fill before a cancellation reaches the server. SL/TP can slip or fail to execute promptly. Daily thresholds are triggers, not guaranteed maximum losses.
- **Bounded management.** At most three cancellation and three closure requests per ticket/action per attachment, at least five observed server seconds apart. Partial closes can leave a residual position after the bound. `MANAGEMENT_DIAGNOSTIC` records/chart text require operator review. Reattaching starts a new bounded management cycle; it never authorizes a new entry attempt for an existing range.
- **Partial entries.** Any observed entry fill causes cancellation of its remaining pending volume on the next management tick. Cancellation may race further fills. Original SL/TP are not deliberately widened or changed; no partial-exit strategy exists. The original full requested monetary risk remains the R denominator, even for a partial fill.
- **Shared identity scope.** The account login/server/magic identity is exclusive across local terminals sharing Windows Common/Files, and history/exposure checks cover its symbols. Remote computers/VPSs do not share an atomic lock. Concurrent same-account operation across machines is unsupported and must be prevented operationally. Changing the magic or deleting risk records creates a different identity and defeats the intended shared limits; neither is a supported way to resume a locked strategy.
- **History and day-start equity.** Successful `HistorySelect` cannot prove the broker retained all historical records. The EA requires available entry history for current positions. It reconstructs day-start account equity from balance/credit and available day cash changes only when history indicates a flat midnight. Otherwise it needs an authoritative, dated override or a later flat day. A corrupt existing daily record is not reset automatically. Unattributable broker charges or missing history can limit EA P&L accuracy.
- **Carried exposure accounting.** Session flattening should normally leave no overnight EA position. If one persists, daily monitoring includes all its current floating P&L plus current-day realized net, conservatively rather than claiming a reconstructed midnight mark. This is not exact incremental midnight-to-now equity attribution for carried positions. Do not supply a guessed day-equity override.
- **Manual and other-EA positions.** Only this magic's strategy-tagged (`BAMD:`) orders and provably exclusive positions can be managed. Same-magic untagged collisions block entries and are not cancelled/closed. Brokers that remove the original order and deal comments can make ownership unprovable and block management. Netting symbols with any existing position/order are rejected for entry. If another actor later adds to the same netting position, the EA locks entries and refuses a discretionary close that could alter foreign ownership. Use a dedicated demo account for controlled testing; simultaneous external activity can race preflight checks.
- **Permission changes.** VisualOnly prevents all trade requests, including management. Changing an instance with active exposure to visual-only, or removing its account permission, prevents its cancellations and session/daily close requests. Broker-attached SL/TP and any broker expiry remain in place; inspect owned exposure before changing modes.
- **Restart recovery is conservative.** Existing pending/open exposure is reconciled from history and durable intent/plan. Unsent signals are invalidated. A missing plan blocks new entries and an unmapped pending entry is cancelled when permission permits. A journal with a truncated footer blocks initialization and needs investigation with trading disabled. There is no fabricated risk denominator for an unknown historical order.
- **News coverage is asserted by the operator.** The sample is fictional. An explicit coverage interval proves configuration only, not that the event source is complete or accurate. Historical surprise/unscheduled events are not filtered. Dynamic calendar filenames require local test-agent installation or a compile-time `tester_file` directive for distribution.
- **Spread, fees and R.** Submission filtering cannot control future fill spread. Notification-time spread is an approximation and unavailable historical observations are marked unknown. Zero cost allowances omit unknown costs from sizing; available actual commissions/swaps/fees still enter realized results. Stop slippage, variable commission schedules and price gaps can exceed planned risk.
- **Report limits.** Equity samples are once per observed minute and account-wide. Sampled drawdown can miss the true maximum and includes external cashflows. Broker wall-clock fallback may make time sorting ambiguous; do not merge different accounts/time bases. Development/validation labels are declarations, not proof that the validation sample was untouched.
- **Performance.** The research ledger rebuilds available full account history approximately once per observed second and after trade notifications. It favors auditable reconciliation over throughput. Very large account histories need profiling and an independently verified incremental ledger before high-volume use. Keep the transaction callback short; it only requests reconciliation on the next tick.
